# Contributing

**Not accepting contributions.** No pull requests, no issues, no feature
requests, no bug reports. Nothing here is a comment on the quality of anyone's
work — this is a single-maintainer project and it stays that way so the
curriculum remains one calibrated instrument rather than a committee's average.

**Not supported.** There is no support channel, no response-time expectation,
and no commitment that anything will be fixed, kept working, or kept existing.

**No warranty.** Use it at your own risk. The software comes as is, with no
guarantee that it is correct, that it will keep running, or that the numbers it
reports mean what you think they mean. See the No Liability section of
[LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md), which is the operative version of this paragraph.

## What you can still do

Read all of it. Run all of it, offline, for free, forever. Fork the _software_
within the terms of [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). Learn from the curriculum without
asking anyone for anything.

That is the deal, and it is not going to get worse.
